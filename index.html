Meet the sisters

Two sisters. One incredible place.

We're sisters, two of five kids, and we've always done things together. Growing up in Princeton, New Jersey, family trips to a fishing and hunting club in the Poconos introduced us to fly fishing at a young age, instilling a love of the outdoors and wide open spaces that has stayed with us ever since.

It was a love of the mountains, fly fishing, and a desire to slow down that ultimately drew us both to Big Sky. Emily arrived first, trading Boston for the mountains in 2015 and building a life here with her husband and three kids. Janie and her family followed in 2017. While skiing wasn't a big part of our lives growing up, it has become a wonderful part of our world here.

Together, we bring complementary strengths to every client relationship. Janie's expertise in the local market, combined with the resources of Big Sky Sotheby's International Realty, means buyers and sellers are supported at every step. Emily keeps every detail organized. We're here to guide you, as a team and as family.
